# My Favorite TV Series

### 1. The 100
A science fiction series about survival, leadership, and rebuilding civilization.

### 2. The Walking Dead
A survival drama about people trying to survive in a world overrun by zombies.

### 3. Dexter
A crime thriller about a forensic analyst who secretly lives a double life.

### 4. Suits
A legal drama about lawyers, friendship, ambition, and competition.

### 5. Grey's Anatomy
A medical drama about doctors, relationships, and the challenges of working in a hospital.

### 6. Shameless
A comedy-drama about the Gallagher family and their unusual everyday lives.

### 7. Breaking Bad
A crime drama about a chemistry teacher whose life takes an unexpected turn.

### 8. You
A psychological thriller about obsession, secrets, and relationships.

### 9. Bridgerton
A romantic period drama about love, family, and high society.

### 10. Behind Her Eyes
A mystery thriller filled with secrets and unexpected plot twists.

### 11. Lupin
A crime series about a clever gentleman thief inspired by Arsène Lupin.

### 12. Lucifer
A fantasy crime drama about the Devil who works with the Los Angeles Police Department.

### 13. Teen Wolf
A supernatural drama about a teenager who becomes a werewolf.
